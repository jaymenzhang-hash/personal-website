02 / About me

Curious about the structures behind everyday life.

I’m Jaymen Zhang, a student pursuing architectural engineering and computer science. I’m interested in how thoughtful engineering and well-designed technology can improve the places, systems, and routines people rely on every day.

Architectural engineering has taught me to think about the built environment as a connected system—where structural decisions, materials, sustainability, and human needs all matter. Computer science gives me another way to explore those ideas: by building useful tools, modeling information clearly, and turning a problem into an experience that people can actually use.

I have experience working with Python and C++, and I enjoy using programming to break down complex problems, test ideas, and create practical projects such as my Calorie Tracker application. I’m especially interested in opportunities that bring together engineering, technology, and real-world impact.

As I continue learning, I’m looking for internships and early-career experiences where I can contribute with curiosity, grow alongside ambitious teams, and help build more sustainable, accessible, and thoughtful solutions.
